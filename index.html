About Us

Welcome to the Center for Creative Learning (CCL) at IIT Gandhinagar. Our mission is to provide clean, accessible, and high-quality learning materials focused on Computational Thinking, AI, Machine Learning, Science, and Mathematics. We believe in learning by doing, breaking down complex concepts into engaging, hands-on experiences.
